What the season looks like from the top 50

Six patterns, drawn from the videos above rather than from commentary around them.

The double reverse four-bar won

It appears across the reveals and has two dedicated breakdowns in this list (#41, and an August one just below the cut). Cascade elevators and differential lifts are the live alternatives.

The skills ceiling doubled in eight weeks

128 points on 18 August (#9), 226 on 22 September (#15), 256 on 12 October (#10). Watching all three back to back is the fastest read on how the meta moved.

Reveals are 24 of the 50

Nearly half the list is 35–100 second team reveals. They cluster in three waves: Mall of America in early August, Speedway in mid-September, and Great Planes, Highlander and Pinnacle through October.

Pits & Parts is the design archive

FUN Robotics Network holds six slots (#3, #4, #13, #19, #33, #42) and covers the robots that go on to win signatures. If you only watch one series, watch this one.

Strategy beat mechanism in September

The highest-ranked analysis piece (#12) argues that fighting for toggles loses, and that role-swapping with your alliance partner matters more than any single mechanism. 471B won Speedway playing that way.

Build tutorials are scarce

Only one video in the fifty (#36) actually teaches a mechanism step by step. Most design knowledge this season is being transmitted through pit interviews rather than tutorials.
